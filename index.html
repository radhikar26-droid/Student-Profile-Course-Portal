Paneer Recipe

Instructions

  • 200 g paneer (Indian cottage cheese)
  • 3 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cream*
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnishing
  • 8–10 cashew nuts (optional)
    •

Method of preparation

Step 1: prepare for the ingredients

Chop the tomatoes and onion. Soak the cashew nuts in warm water for 10 minutes and blend them with the tomatoes and onion into a smooth paste.



Step 2: Heat the butter

Heat oil and butter in a pan. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for one minute.


Step 3: Cook the gravy

Add the prepared paste, chilli powder, turmeric powder, coriander powder, and salt. Cook for 8–10 minutes.




Step 4: Add the paneer

Add paneer cubes and one cup of water. Simmer for 5–10 minutes until the gravy becomes thick.



Step 5: Add cream and garnish

Add fresh cream and mix gently. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and turn off the heat.



3. Serving

Serve hot Paneer Butter Masala with roti, naan, or steamed rice. Enjoy your delicious homemade meal!
